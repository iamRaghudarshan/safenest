The first time you open it, your computer may warn you

This app runs entirely on your own machine and is not distributed through an app store, so Windows and macOS ask you to confirm you trust it. That is expected.

  1. Windows: if you see “Windows protected your PC”, click More info → Run anyway.
  2. Mac: if it offers only Move to Bin, right-click the app → Open → Open. If it still refuses, run in Terminal: xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/<AppName>.app

🍎  Mac — Apple Silicon (M1 or newer), macOS 11+

Open Terminal (press ⌘ + Space, type “Terminal”, Return), then paste this one line and press Return:

Apple blocks apps it hasn’t personally reviewed, so a normal download shows a “damaged” warning. This one line installs SafeNest safely and opens it — nothing to click through.

Before you ask

The questions everyone asks

If I stop paying, do I lose everything?+

No. Your records are files on your own computer, and they stay there. The app also exports everything you own — every account, every photo and document — into a folder you can copy onto a USB drive, at any time, including after you have stopped.

Can you read my records?+

No, and not as a policy — as a matter of plumbing. We run no server that holds them. There is nothing for us to look in, nothing to lose in a breach of ours, and nothing we could be compelled to hand over.

What happens if my computer dies?+

Take the copy to the next one. Profile → Move everything to another computer writes a folder with every record and file in it; the new machine picks up exactly where the old one stopped. Keeping one on an external drive is the five-minute version of a disaster plan.

Do I need to be technical to run it?+

No. It installs everything it needs the first time you open it — no database to set up, no account to create, nothing to configure. Reaching it from outside your home is the one optional step that takes longer, and the app walks you through it.

Can my family have their own sign-ins?+

Yes, as many as your licence covers. Everyone gets their own records and nobody can see anyone else’s — a household, not a shared login.

Does it work on my phone?+

Yes. Install the phone app, type in your address, sign in with the same details. On your own Wi-Fi it just works; from outside the house you need a web address, which the app helps you set up. The computer has to be switched on — your records live on it, not on a server of ours.
