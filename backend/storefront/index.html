🧩 Windows
⬇ Download for Windows

If Windows shows “protected your PC”, click More info → Run anyway — that is expected for any app not sold through a store.

 Mac — Apple Silicon (M1 or newer), macOS 11+

Open Terminal (⌘ + Space, type “Terminal”, Return), paste this one line and press Return:

curl -fsSL /install-mac.sh | bash

Apple blocks apps it has not personally reviewed, so a normal download shows a “damaged” warning. This one line installs SafeNest safely and opens it.

The questions everyone asks

Where is my data actually kept?

In a database file on your own computer, in a folder the app shows you. You can copy it, back it up to your own drive, or move it to another machine. We never receive it.

Does it work without the internet?

Yes. Everything opens, searches and saves offline. A connection is needed once, to activate your licence.

Is there really no limit on photos and scans?

There is no limit imposed by SafeNest. Files are written to your own disk at full resolution, so the only limit is how much free space that disk has.

What happens if I change computers?

Copy your data file across and activate SafeNest on the new machine. Nothing has to be re-entered.

Can more than one person in the house use it?

Yes — the household can share one SafeNest, with separate sign-ins.

What if I forget my sign-in?

Because there is no account with us, we cannot reset it for you — that is the direct cost of us not holding your data. Keep your recovery details somewhere safe.
